Part 2: 2001 Nobel Prize in Physics, Bose-Einstein condensation, and the atom laser.
Bose-Einstein condensation and the atom laser show countless atoms moving as one wave.

Eric A. Cornell, Wolfgang Ketterle, and Carl E. Wieman won.
Room air is about 300 K and space 3 K; rubidium began condensing at about 170 nK in Boulder in June 1995.
Cold atoms' waves overlap, gathering into one lowest-energy wave and moving as one.
Ketterle overlapped two condensates to reveal wave interference stripes and drew out atoms to make an atom laser.
