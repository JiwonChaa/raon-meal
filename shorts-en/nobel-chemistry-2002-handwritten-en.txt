Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 3 (2002)
Soft ionization lets mass spectrometry study large biological molecules with less fragmentation.
Fenn and Tanaka advanced these ionization methods, which allow measurements of mass-to-charge ratios. Wüthrich used NMR signals and distance constraints to determine protein structures in solution.
