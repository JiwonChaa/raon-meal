Palladium-catalyzed cross coupling: the 2010 Chemistry Nobel.
The Suzuki reaction joins selected carbons from two molecules.
Starting-material positions guide where the new bond forms. Organic groups pass through palladium intermediates before the coupled product leaves and the catalyst is regenerated. Negishi and Heck reactions use different partners or pathways.
