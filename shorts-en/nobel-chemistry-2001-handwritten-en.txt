Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 2 (2001)
Chiral catalysts make one reaction pathway faster, producing more of one mirror-image molecular form.
Knowles and Noyori developed methods for hydrogenation, while Sharpless developed methods for oxidation. These reactions help chemists make the desired mirror-image form.
