Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 5 (2004)
Proteasomes recognize ubiquitin chains that signal protein degradation.
They unfold the tagged protein and cut it into fragments. Ubiquitin is removed and reused. Different ways of linking ubiquitin can also convey other cellular signals.
