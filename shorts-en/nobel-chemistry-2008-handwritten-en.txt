Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 9 (2008)
Green fluorescent protein, GFP, can tag a target protein inside living cells.
Suitable light makes GFP fluoresce, revealing where the tagged protein gathers and moves. The image shows the tag's distribution. Researchers must check that the tag preserves the target protein's normal function and location.
