2026 Nobel Medicine: Deisseroth, Hegemann and Nagel's optogenetics.
Channelrhodopsin lets light control neurons.

Chlamydomonas channelrhodopsin opens an ion pore in light. In 2005, its gene enabled blue light to trigger neuronal spikes. Targeting cell types tests links between neural activity and behavior. In 2021, one patient with retinitis pigmentosa partly regained vision.
