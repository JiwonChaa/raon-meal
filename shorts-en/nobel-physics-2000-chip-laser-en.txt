2000 Nobel Prize in Physics, part 1: Integrated circuits and Semiconductor lasers.
Kilby, Alferov, and Kroemer's work gave us chips and optical communication lasers.

Late-1947 transistors saved space and power, but wiring over 10,000 was hard. Kilby ran a germanium circuit in September 1958. The 1962 laser could not stay on at room temperature; in 1963, Alferov and Kroemer independently proposed layers trapping electrons and light. In May 1970, Alferov's team achieved continuous room-temperature lasing for fiber optics, CDs, and barcode readers.
