2024 Chemistry Nobel: protein structure prediction and computational design.
AlphaFold2 predicts structure from sequence; Rosetta helps choose sequences for a target structure.

Amino-acid order and interactions shape a folded protein. Predictions learn from experimental data, while designed sequences must be made and tested to establish whether the intended structure forms.
