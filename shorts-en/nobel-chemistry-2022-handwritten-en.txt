2022 Chemistry Nobel: click chemistry and bioorthogonal chemistry.
Azides and strained alkynes make it possible to label cell-surface glycans.

Copper catalysis accelerates molecular coupling. Ring strain offers a copper-free route compatible with living cells, while fluorescent tags reveal where the labeled glycans are.
