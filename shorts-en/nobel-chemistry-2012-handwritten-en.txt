G-protein-coupled receptors and cell signaling: the 2012 Chemistry Nobel.
An external binding event changes a receptor's shape and activates an internal G protein.
GDP leaves before GTP binds, allowing the G protein to influence enzymes or ion channels. Lefkowitz tracked receptors, while Kobilka studied their genes and active structures.
