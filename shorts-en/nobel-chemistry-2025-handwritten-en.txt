2025 Chemistry Nobel: metal–organic frameworks and water harvesting.
Designed pore surfaces adsorb vapor, then heating releases it for collection by condensation.

Metal centers and organic linkers create internal surfaces that can capture selected molecules. Suitable water-harvesting frameworks repeat adsorption and thermal release, while a separate cooler surface collects liquid water.
