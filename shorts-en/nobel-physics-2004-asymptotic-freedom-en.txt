Part 5: 2004 Nobel Prize in Physics and Asymptotic freedom.
Strong interaction: quarks feel less force as they get closer.

A proton's 3 quarks move almost freely, yet none escapes alone. Gluons gather into a thin cord, binding quarks like a rubber band. In 1973, David J. Gross and graduate student Frank Wilczek, and graduate student H. David Politzer working independently, calculated this effect. Harder collisions confirmed the weaker force at closer range, earning them the 2004 prize.
