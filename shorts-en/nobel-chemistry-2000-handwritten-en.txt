Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 1 (2000)
Iodine accepts electrons from polyacetylene, leaving mobile regions of positive charge along its chains.
This doping lets the same-sized film carry more current at the same voltage. Charge transfer between chains also affects the current in a real film.
