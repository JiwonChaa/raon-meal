Part 3 covers the 2002 Nobel Prize in Physics: Solar Neutrinos and Cosmic X-rays.
Davis and Koshiba detected solar neutrinos; Giacconi found cosmic X-rays.

Hydrogen fusing into helium emits neutrinos that reach Earth. Davis used 615 t of dry-cleaning fluid 1,500 m down a gold mine, yielding about 17 argon atoms every two months. His 1970–1994 data supported solar fusion, and Koshiba traced neutrinos to the Sun using light in water. Giacconi found cosmic X-rays with a rocket detector.
