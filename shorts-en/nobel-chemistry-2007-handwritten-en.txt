Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 8 (2007)
Surface catalysis involves adsorption, reaction on the surface, and desorption of the product.
On platinum, adsorbed carbon monoxide reacts with oxygen atoms to form carbon dioxide. Too much carbon monoxide can block sites needed by oxygen and slow the reaction. Ertl's experiments connected these surface conditions to reaction rates.
