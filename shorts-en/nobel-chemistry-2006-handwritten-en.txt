Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 7 (2006)
During transcription, RNA polymerase uses one DNA strand as a template to build RNA.
Roger Kornberg determined structures of yeast RNA polymerase II, including one bound to DNA and growing RNA. These structures showed how the enzyme holds the strands and carries out RNA synthesis.
