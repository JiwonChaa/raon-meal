Chemistry Nobel Prizes of the 21st Century, Episode 6 (2005)
Olefin metathesis exchanges the molecular parts on either side of carbon-carbon double bonds.
A metal carbene and an olefin form a four-atom ring. The ring opens in a different direction, releasing a new olefin and another metal carbene. Repeating this cycle rearranges carbon skeletons.
